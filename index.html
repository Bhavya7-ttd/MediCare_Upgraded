🔒 MediCare Privacy Policy

1. Data Ownership & Storage: Your personal health records, medicine schedules, appointments, and prescriptions are stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore and Firebase Storage associated strictly with your authenticated user account.

2. Caregiver Access: Patient data is only accessible to authorized caregivers after explicit link confirmation via 24-hour single-use caregiver codes. Access can be revoked at any time.

3. AI Assistant: Health questions submitted via the AI Assistant are processed securely by Google Gemini API. No identifiable personal health information is sold or shared with third parties.

4. Data Deletion: You retain full control to permanently delete your account and all associated Firestore documents, Storage files, and device tokens at any time using the "Delete My Account" feature in Caregiver/Account settings.
